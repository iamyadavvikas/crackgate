## 2. WCL Mining Sirdar Exam Pattern

The WCL Mining Sirdar exam is divided into two parts as per the official syllabus (Annexure-I). While marks distribution may vary slightly as per the latest notification, aspirants must prepare strictly as per this structure.

| Part | Subjects Covered | Focus Area | Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Part 1** | General English, Reasoning (Verbal & Non-Verbal), Logical & Analytical Ability, Quantitative Aptitude, Data Interpretation, General Awareness (Current Affairs + Coal Industry) | Aptitude + GK | Objective/MCQ Based |

## 4. Part 2 Syllabus – Technical Mining Subjects

### 4.1 Explosives & Blasting Practice (Opencast & Underground)

- Types of explosives used in coal mines
- Blasting accessories – detonators (Electric/Non-Electric), safety fuse, detonating cord
- Blasting patterns in OC & UG
- Controlled blasting, deck charging, stemming, burden & spacing
- Precautions before, during & after blasting
- Misfires – causes, handling, precautions & remedial measures

### 4.2 Mining Methods (Underground)

- **Board & Pillar Development:** Panel system, dimensions, cycle of operations
- **Depillaring:** Methods, line of goaf, extraction sequence & precautions
- **Mechanised Methods & Mass Production Technology:** Continuous Miner, SDL, LHD, mechanised pillar extraction
- **Air Blast:** Causes, prevention & control
- **Contiguous Working:** Definition, conditions & safety precautions

### 4.3 Strata Control, Support & Timbering

- **Strata Control & Monitoring Plan (SCAMP):** Objectives, instrumentation & implementation
- **Roof Bolting:** Cement grout vs Resin bolting, bolt length, spacing & installation
- **Anchorage Testing of Roof Bolts:** Importance, methods & acceptance criteria
- **Quality of Support:** Inspection & record keeping
- **Withdrawal of Supports:** Systematic withdrawal, dangers & precautions
- **Timbering:** Types, setting, renewal & safe withdrawal

### 4.4 Stowing (Backfilling)

- Purpose of stowing in UG coal mines
- Methods – Hydraulic, Pneumatic & Mechanical
- Stowing ratio, difficulties & remedial measures
- Materials used for stowing/filling
- Subsidence control through stowing

### 4.5 Opencast (OC) Mining

- **Methods:** Manual, Semi-mechanised & Fully mechanised
- **Deep Hole Drilling & Blasting:** Design, safety aspects
- **HEMM:** Shovel-Dumper combination, Dragline, Surface Miner – working & applications
- **Benching:** Formation, height, width, slope & maintenance
- **Haul Roads:** Construction, drainage, maintenance & safety
- **Extraction of Developed Pillars by OC Method:** Precautions & statutory aspects
- **Dump Management:** Formation, stability & compaction
- **Bench & Dump Slope Monitoring:** Purpose, methods & early warning signs
- **Traffic Rules & Regulations, LMV Roads**

### 4.6 Drifting

- Purpose, gradient, dimensions & location
- Manual vs Mechanised drifting
- Support, ventilation & blasting in drifts

### 4.7 Geology

- Formation of coal – Peatification, Coalification & Rank of coal
- Geological disturbances – Faults, Folds, Dykes, Joints, Washouts
- Effects of geological disturbances on mining & precautions

### 4.8 Mine Surveying (UG & OC)

- Basic principles of surveying
- Instruments – Level, Theodolite, Total Station & EDM (Basics)
- Levelling, traversing & contouring
- Plans & Sections – Working Plan, Ventilation Plan, Rescue Plan, Surface Plan, Geological Plan
- Survey checks, benchmarks & closures

### 4.9 Danger of Inundation

- Dangers in UG & Opencast mines
- Approaching water-logged workings – precautions, probing & advance boreholes
- Water dams, bulkheads & their functions
- Relevant provisions of Coal Mines Regulations, 2017 (CMR 2017)

### 4.10 Mine Fires (Surface & Underground)

- Causes – Spontaneous heating, friction, electrical, blasting & open flames
- Surface fires, UG fires & coal stack fires
- Fire detection, prevention & fire-fighting techniques
- **Isolation Stopping:** Construction, purpose & sealing
- Fittings, inspection & records

### 4.11 Mine Rescue

- Mine Rescue Organisation, Rescue Stations & Apparatus
- Self-Contained Breathing Apparatus (SCBA) – basics
- Emergency preparedness, rescue procedures
- Role & responsibilities of Sirdar during emergencies

### 4.12 Mine Ventilation & Mine Gases

- Objectives of ventilation – dilution, removal & cooling
- Natural & mechanical ventilation
- Ventilation systems – splitting, ventilation pressure
- **Mine Gases:** CH₄, CO, CO₂, H₂S, O₂ deficiency – properties, detection, effects & explosive ranges
- Gas detection instruments – Flame Safety Lamp, Methanometer, CO Detector, Multi-gas Detector
- Gas Chromatography & Air Sampling – basics & purpose
- Gas explosions – causes, prevention & general concept

### 4.13 Duties of Mining Sirdar

- General duties, supervision & maintenance of discipline
- Inspection – quality, frequency, systematic approach
- Persons working under him & work allocation
- Charge handover/takeover – procedure & importance
- **Report Book:** Proper entries, accuracy, timeliness & statutory compliance

### 4.14 Man-Riding Systems in UG Mines

- Purpose, types & essential safety features
- Man-riding cars, chairlifts, rope haulage for men
- Statutory precautions & inspections

### 4.15 Winding, Haulage, Roadways, Lighting & Means of Access

- **Shaft Winding:** Types, components & safety devices (Detaching hook, Overwind preventor, Brakes, Depth indicators)
- **Roadways:** Haulage roadways, Travelling roadways, Belt roadways – construction, clearances & safety
- **Lighting:** Cap lamps, flameproof lighting, general lighting
- **Whitewashing:** Purpose & importance for safety
- **Means of Access & Egress:** Escapeways & statutory requirements

### 4.16 Shaft Sinking

- Methods of shaft sinking – Conventional & Mechanised (basics)
- Temporary & permanent support
- Shaft lining – purpose, types (Brick, Concrete, Steel) & procedure
- Safety precautions during shaft sinking

### 4.17 Coal Dust – Regulations, Precautions & Control

- **Hazards:** Coal dust explosions & health effects (Pneumoconiosis)
- **Dust Suppression:** Water sprays, wet drilling, dust extractors
- **Stone Dusting:** Purpose, frequency, application & sampling
- **Stone Dust Barriers:** Types, location, construction & effectiveness
- **Dust Sampling:** Gravimetric Dust Sampler & Personal Dust Sampler (PDS) – working principle & significance
- Relevant CMR 2017 provisions on dust control

### 4.18 Accident – Classification, Causes & Prevention

- Classification – Fatal, Serious, Reportable, Dangerous Occurrence & Near Miss
- Near Miss – reporting, investigation & role in proactive safety
- Causes – direct/indirect, root causes
- Accident prevention, reporting & remedial measures

### 4.19 Face Machineries & Equipment

- **Loading Equipment:** SDL (Side Discharge Loader), LHD (Load-Haul-Dumper), Continuous Miner & its package
- **Belt Conveyor:** Components, drives, safety devices, maintenance & fire precautions
- **Drill Machines:** Underground Drill Machines (UDM) & face drills
- **Haulage:** Main & tail rope, endless rope, locomotives (basics), safety aspects
- **Haulage Ropes:** Inspection, care & discard criteria
- **Face Pumps:** Purpose, installation & maintenance

### 4.20 Electrical – Equipment, Safety & Devices

- Flameproof (FLP) enclosures & Intrinsically Safe (IS) apparatus
- Electrical cables, switches, junction boxes & gate-end boxes
- Electrical hazards in gassy/dusty mines & precautions
- Earthing, protective devices & statutory checks
- Approved electrical equipment for use in UG mines

### 4.21 Provisions of Acts, Rules, Bye-Laws & DGMS Circulars

- **Mines Act, 1952:** Key sections on health, safety, welfare, hours of work, inspectors & penalties
- **Coal Mines Regulations, 2017 (CMR 2017):** Must-focus – Safety, Ventilation, Support, Blasting, Winding, Haulage, Electrical, Dust, Inspections & Duties of Officials
- **Mines Rules, 1955:** Relevant provisions on medical, welfare, registers, notices & returns
- **Bye-Laws:** Purpose, framing, approval & enforcement
- **DGMS Circulars:** Latest circulars relating to safety of persons employed in mines & duties of Sirdar/Shotfirer (Check [dgms.gov.in](https://dgms.gov.in/) regularly)

### 4.22 Writing of Reports

- **Types:** Shift reports, Inspection reports, Accident/Near-miss reports, Handover reports & basic returns
- **Essentials:** Clarity, factual accuracy, objectivity, completeness & timeliness
- **Correct terminology & use of mine forms/registers**
- Do’s & Don’ts of effective technical report writing

## 7. WCL Mining Sirdar Exam Preparation Strategy (90-Day Plan)

### Phase 1: Concept Building (Days 1–30)

- **Technical Focus:** Cover Explosives & Blasting, Mining Methods, Support & Timbering, Ventilation, Surveying & Geology.
- **Aptitude:** Solve 20–30 questions daily (Simplification, Number System, Reasoning).
- **GK & Current Affairs:** Read for 30–40 minutes daily (Focus on Coal Sector + National News).
- **Revision:** Revise what you studied the previous day.

### Phase 2: Deep Coverage + Revision (Days 31–60)

- **High-Yield Topics:** Prioritise CMR 2017, Mines Act 1952, DGMS Circulars, Mine Fires, Inundation, Coal Dust, Face Machinery & Electrical.
- **MCQ Practice:** Attempt 50–60 topic-wise MCQs daily.
- **Mocks:** Take 1 mini-mock every 2–3 days & analyse mistakes.
- **Weak Areas:** Revisit Phase 1 topics where accuracy is below 80%.

### Phase 3: Full Revision, Mocks & Speed Building (Days 61–90)

- **Full-Length Mocks:** Attempt 1–2 mocks daily under exam-like conditions.
- **Error Notebook:** Revise all incorrect questions daily without fail.
- **Rapid Revision:** Use your short notes for Gases, Formulas, Sections of Acts, SCAMP, Stowing & Ventilation.
- **PYQs:** Solve maximum WCL Mining Sirdar previous year questions.
- **Fine-tune:** Focus on time management, accuracy & avoiding silly errors.

## 8. Tips to Crack WCL Mining Sirdar Exam in First Attempt

- **Stick to the Official Syllabus:** Treat the Annexure-I as your ultimate roadmap.
- **Give Priority to Part 2:** Allocate 65–75% of your study time to technical + mining legislation.
- **Prepare Short Notes:** Make crisp notes for CMR 2017, Mine Gases, Ventilation, Formulas, Definitions & DGMS provisions. Revise them at least thrice a week.
- **Solve Previous Year Questions:** Identify repeating topics and question patterns from past WCL Mining Sirdar papers.
- **Practice Time-Bound Mocks:** Aim for 90%+ accuracy consistently. Speed comes with regular practice.
- **Master Mining Safety & Statutory:** Focus heavily on Duties of Sirdar, CMR 2017, DGMS Circulars, Accidents & Near-Miss reporting.
- **Stay Updated:** Regularly check [westerncoal.in](https://westerncoal.in/) & [coalindia.in](https://coalindia.in/) for latest notifications & exam updates.
- **Use the Error Notebook:** Review every mistake till you never repeat it.
- **Focus on Application-Based Questions:** Pay extra attention to Blasting, Support, SCAMP, Stowing, Slope Monitoring & Ventilation numericals/concepts.
- **Stay Consistent:** 3–4 focused hours daily for 90 days beats irregular 10-hour marathons.

**Q1. What is the full form of WCL Mining Sirdar T&S Grade-C?**

A: WCL – Western Coalfields Limited, T&S – Time and Scale, Grade-C – the designated grade for the Mining Sirdar post.
